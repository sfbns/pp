# Specialist orchestration contract

## Handoff schema

Every specialist memo must contain:

1. `question_received` — the exact decision or uncertainty it addresses;
2. `source_scope` — private memory and paper cards used, with evidence status;
3. `answer` — the substantive recommendation;
4. `mechanism_chain` — agents, actions, constraints, timing, and each causal arrow;
5. `predictions` — proposition, empirical hypothesis, heterogeneity or boundary prediction;
6. `rival_and_falsifier` — closest alternative and discriminating evidence;
7. `handoff` — unresolved question for the next specialist;
8. `confidence_and_limits` — what is verified, inferred, or missing.

## Full-task sequence

### Wave 1: independent grounding

- `literature_scout`: retrieve the closest paper cards and verify source status.
- `economic_foundations_professor`: identify the canonical benchmark and primitive held fixed.
- `policy_background_professor`: audit realized implementation, gatekeepers, discretion, and treatment versions when relevant.
- `idea_generator`: offer at most three corrected objects or wedges when the research object is not yet fixed.

### Wave 2: theory contest

- `theory_professor_1`: derive the strongest baseline extension and comparative statics.
- `theory_professor_2`: derive the strongest rival, boundary conditions, and discriminating patterns independently.

Do not show one theory agent the other's conclusion before its independent memo when the task is testing robustness.

### Wave 3: hypothesis bridge

- `hypothesis_packager`: reconcile the benchmark, wedge, mechanisms, observables, rivals, and source-backed rhetorical pattern into a hypothesis ladder.
- `formal_model_designer`: build the smallest model that earns the claims, if equations or equilibrium restrictions are needed.
- `structural_professor`: define a named counterfactual, parameters, identification, computation, and validation only if reduced form is insufficient.
- `blp_professor`: handle differentiated-products demand and supply only when BLP is substantively required.

### Wave 4: empirical and prose audit

- `identification_referee`: separate theory predictions from causal identification, audit the estimand, threats, and falsification design.
- `writing_integrator`: write the paper-ready logic without strengthening evidence or hiding rivals.
- `economics_theory_agent`: adjudicate conflicts, state which mechanism survives, and own the final answer.

## Conflict rules

- A canonical benchmark does not establish empirical relevance.
- A mechanism-consistent pattern does not establish mediation or causality.
- A statistically identified reduced-form effect does not identify structural primitives.
- Structural-model flexibility does not rescue weak measurement or absent parameter variation.
- A top-journal analogy is a design prior, never novelty proof.
- When specialist memos conflict, retain both claims until a stated assumption, observable, or falsification test resolves them.
