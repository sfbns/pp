## Reusable idea-generation actions

1. [inference] **Corrected-object canvas**: fill six cells—visible problem, familiar object, missing component, corrected object, changed decision, observable consequence.
2. [inference] **Frozen-primitive inversion**: take one benchmark assumption and ask which agent could choose it, with what information, at what time, and under which constraint.
3. [inference] **Scalar-to-vector expansion**: test whether price, quality, treatment, effort, or welfare actually has separable components with distinct incidence.
4. [inference] **Complementary-market inventory**: list every market or institution required to execute the focal action—credit, lending, transmission, housing, land, labor, compliance, or approval.
5. [inference] **Two-friction crossing matrix**: write four worlds—neither friction, F only, G only, both—and require the joint world to generate a new signed or threshold prediction.
6. [inference] **Endogenous-boundary scan**: ask whether agents can alter coverage, category, ownership, site, segment, workforce, case mix, or timing.
7. [inference] **Observability hunt**: search for a special institutional arrangement that reveals beliefs, counterfactual decisions, shadow prices, menus, or latent constraints.
8. [inference] **Rival-first discriminator**: before celebrating an idea, write the closest rival and one distribution/timing/heterogeneity pattern on which they disagree.
9. [inference] **Minimum-data sketch**: name unit, treatment/shock, direct primitive measure, action margin, outcome, timing, and source. “Large dataset” is not a data plan.
10. [inference] **Kill-before-scale rule**: reject the candidate if the wedge changes no optimization/equilibrium object, has no distinct observable, duplicates the nearest correction, or requires unidentified structure for its only claim.

## Failure modes and evidence boundaries

- [inference] Do not generate “X affects Y” ideas with theory vocabulary added afterward.
- [inference] Do not call a demographic or regional label a mechanism; identify the choice-margin primitive it indexes.
- [inference] Do not add an interaction term unless the joint friction changes a constraint, residual supply/demand slope, or equilibrium response.
- [inference] Do not choose a moderator after observing a sign switch; the state variable must be theory-prior.
- [inference] Do not treat an omitted outcome as important unless it changes the total object or policy ranking.
- [inference] Do not confuse “can be modeled” with “needs a model.”
- [inference] Do not force BLP because products or prices appear; differentiated substitution, endogenous prices, shares, instruments, and a demand/supply counterfactual must be central.
- [bridge-card synthesis] Do not upgrade a bridge-card pattern to a paper-level factual claim. Exact conditions and source anchors require reopening.
- [inference] Do not preserve an idea merely because it is novel if the institution cannot deliver the treatment or the data cannot distinguish the mechanism.
