# Results, discussion, and conclusion audit route

For section-craft tasks involving result exposition, discussion, conclusions, environmental inequality, or governance-resource allocation, invoke `learn-academic-writing` and read:

`..\..\learn-academic-writing\references\results-discussion-conclusion-craft-2026-08-13.md`

Use `wd-journal-english-writing` for World Development English and `chinese-economics-academic-writing` for Chinese prose. Invoke `econometrics-expert` before changing any identification, estimand, inference, or mechanism judgment.

Audit in this order:

1. claim object and evidence level;
2. preferred specification, comparison, unit, denominator, population, and period;
3. main result plus important null or failure;
4. mechanism grade and unresolved rival;
5. literature relation: alignment, strength, object, mechanism, or identification revision;
6. distribution, institutional responsibility, and scope;
7. environmental-inequality or resource-misallocation benchmark;
8. compression only after the preceding jobs survive.

Treat the 30-paper corpus as writing evidence. Reopen an anchored source packet for paper-specific claims, and do not promote section-level reading to full-paper certification.

For government environmental-information visibility, additionally audit:

9. government level and fact object;
10. monitor/site existence versus actual capture, quality-control retention, valid submission, and authorised access;
11. `R^G` raw government input versus qualified `V^G` versus downstream `A`;
12. outward transparency and political salience versus acquisition of new pollution facts;
13. centre and edge effects separately, plus a formal difference;
14. intervention-chain de-duplication across related publications.
