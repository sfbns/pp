## Story Logic
- The paper argues that fuel-tax and emissions policies helped diffuse diesel vehicles in Europe.
- It shows that those policies favored diesel in ways that also acted like non-tariff trade barriers.
- The policy implication is that environmental rules can generate competitive effects that look like protectionism.
- Real-world tension or puzzle: diesel diffusion was not just a technology story; policy was shaping relative advantage.
- Why the puzzle matters now: climate and trade policy often interact in hidden ways.

- What is still missing or weakly identified: a structural estimate of the trade-policy equivalent of those effects.
- The paper's move: use a structural model to translate policy-induced diesel diffusion into NTB equivalents.
- Main payoff: emissions policy can function like an implicit trade barrier.
