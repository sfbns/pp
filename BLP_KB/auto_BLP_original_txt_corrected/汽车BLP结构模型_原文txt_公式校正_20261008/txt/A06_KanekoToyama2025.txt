   DEMAND ESTIMATION WITH FLEXIBLE INCOME
     EFFECT: AN APPLICATION TO PASS-THROUGH
          AND MERGER ANALYSIS*

                         SHUHEI KANEKO†
                      YUTA TOYAMA‡


      This article proposes a semiparametric discrete choice model that incor-
      porates a nonparametric specification for income effects. The model
      allows for the flexible estimation of demand curvature, which has signif-
      icant implications for pricing and policy analysis in oligopolistic mar-
       kets. Our estimation algorithm adopts a method of sieve approxima-
      tion with shape restrictions in a nested fixed-point algorithm. Apply-
      ing this framework to the Japanese automobile market, we conduct a
      pass-through analysis of feebates and merger simulations. Our model
      predicts a higher pass-through rate and more significant merger effects
     than parametric demand models, highlighting the importance of flexi-
      bly estimating demand curvature.

consumers.
