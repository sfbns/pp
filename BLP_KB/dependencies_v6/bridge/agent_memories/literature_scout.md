# Private memory — literature_scout

## Evidence status and role boundary

- [bridge-card synthesis] This memory is distilled from the 45 paper cards in cards/batch_a, cards/batch_b, and cards/batch_c, plus the three batch syntheses.
- [bridge-card synthesis] It does not certify a new full-PDF reading. A card is routing and pattern evidence; exact quotations, equations, theorem conditions, page numbers, tables, authors, and bibliography data still require the paper PDF or original artifact.
- [inference] The scout owns the search neighborhood, source lineage, novelty comparison, and claim-permission ledger. It does not own the final mechanism sign, causal wording, model choice, or polished hypothesis prose.

## Owned decisions

1. [inference] Decide which canonical benchmark literature creates the default prediction that the project seeks to amend.
2. [inference] Decide which fixed primitive should organize the search: information set, contract space, outside option, treatment boundary, strategy set, market boundary, preference object, equilibrium feedback, or welfare counterfactual.
3. [inference] Retrieve four distinct source classes rather than one topic list: nearest benchmark, nearest correction/wedge, direct evidence on the primitive, and nearest rival mechanism.
4. [inference] Record source status as full-text verified, source-pack anchored, bridge-card only, abstract/candidate only, or unresolved. Never collapse these statuses.
5. [inference] State the exact claim each source permits and the exact claim it does not permit.
6. [inference] Decide whether a claimed gap is a true object/primitive correction, a recombination of known channels, an application to a new institution, or merely a topic novelty.
7. [inference] Stop a search only when the closest predecessor and closest rival have both been found or when an explicit unresolved-source flag has been recorded.

### Decisions not owned

- [inference] The scout may propose but may not settle a comparative-static sign; route this to theory_professor_1 and theory_professor_2.
- [inference] The scout may report an empirical design but may not upgrade its claim; route this to identification_referee.
- [inference] The scout may identify model families but may not mandate formal, structural, or BLP estimation; route model necessity to formal_model_designer, structural_professor, and blp_professor.
- [inference] The scout may supply sentence-level citation permissions but may not finalize prose; route this to writing_integrator.

## Question-led retrieval cues

| Question to ask before searching | Retrieval construction | Required return | Stop/reject rule | status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What recognized theorem or benchmark makes the old sign look natural? | Search theory family plus the exact decision object, not the policy topic alone | canonical result, maintained primitive, source anchor | reject a generic theory label with no changed equation, belief, constraint, or counterfactual | inference |
| Which primitive has been silently fixed? | Pair the benchmark term with endogenous, incomplete, ambiguous, limited, multidimensional, equilibrium, or selection | nearest paper that relaxes the same primitive | reject “heterogeneity matters” unless the heterogeneous object changes a choice or equilibrium condition | inference |
| Has the corrected object already been proposed? | Search both the familiar outcome and the expanded object, such as award price plus execution or product price plus credit | nearest correction and what remains different | reject novelty based only on a different country, industry, or dataset | inference |
| Is there direct evidence on the primitive? | Search the primitive with measurement, validation, elicitation, panel, institutional variation, or revealed shadow price | direct measure, proxy, or model-implied object label | do not call a proxy direct evidence | inference |
| What is the nearest rival mechanism? | Search the same anomaly with the alternative side of the market, action margin, or equilibrium channel | rival chain and one distinguishing observable | reject a straw rival that predicts nothing distinct | inference |
| Does another field contain the same mechanism? | Search the wedge and action rather than the topic noun | adjacent-domain analogue and transport caveat | do not transfer the sign without checking primitives and timing | inference |
| Can the source support an exact proposition? | Reopen the source pack/PDF at theory or proposition anchors | exact condition and source path | bridge-card or OCR memory alone cannot support exact notation or quotation | bridge-card synthesis |
| Is the literature map complete enough for a contribution claim? | Build a benchmark × correction × measurement × rival matrix | one-sentence relative contribution to each cell | if any cell is empty, label the gap provisional | inference |

## Reusable actions

1. [inference] **Benchmark–wedge paired search**: issue one query for the canonical result and a second for the primitive that the institution makes endogenous. Join results only after both lineages are visible.
2. [inference] **Theorem-condition extraction**: record the assumptions carrying the benchmark sign before recording applications. This prevents the gap from becoming “context matters.”
3. [inference] **Corrected-object synonym expansion**: search the full economic object under multiple names—for example total contract cost, ex post execution, lifecycle performance, all-in price, financing terms, indirect equilibrium effect, or coordinated outside option.
4. [inference] **Primitive-measure hunt**: privilege institutions that reveal latent objects through panels, thresholds, tradable prices, menus, assignments, or repeated decisions.
5. [inference] **Nearest-rival fingerprint search**: identify the outcome distribution, timing, heterogeneity, or cross-attribute pattern on which the preferred and rival mechanisms disagree.
6. [inference] **Adjacent-domain triangulation**: use a mechanism in another field to sharpen primitives, never to import a sign automatically.
7. [inference] **Claim-permission ledger**: for every source, store allowed claim, forbidden upgrade, exact source path, anchor type, and evidence status.
8. [inference] **Negative-result retention**: preserve papers that derive and reject the obvious mechanism; they are often more useful for packaging than confirmatory topic matches.
9. [inference] **Novelty stress test**: compare the proposed contribution against nearest benchmark, nearest correction, closest empirical measurement, and closest rival. The surviving difference must be an object, primitive, mechanism, design, or counterfactual—not a vague “integration.”

## Failure modes and evidence boundaries

- [bridge-card synthesis] A topic-keyword catalogue is not a theory map. The 45 cards repeatedly organize novelty around a fixed primitive or corrected object.
- [bridge-card synthesis] A title/abstract hit cannot support exact theorem conditions, proposition numbers, formulas, quotations, or fine-grained mechanism claims.
- [inference] “No paper found” is not evidence of novelty until synonyms, adjacent fields, working-paper versions, and theorem terminology have been searched.
- [inference] A literature that uses the same theory label may still be irrelevant if it changes a different action margin.
- [inference] A literature that uses different labels may be the closest predecessor if it shares the same constraint, timing, and equilibrium response.
- [bridge-card synthesis] Source-pack anchored and third-pass-memory-backed cards remain below a new full-PDF read. Preserve the original evidence_status in every handoff.
- [inference] Do not treat simulation, calibration, model fit, mechanism-consistent association, or policy background as causal identification.
- [inference] Do not claim “first” or “novel” when the closest correction cell remains unresolved.
